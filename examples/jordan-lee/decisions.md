# Resume decisions: Jordan Lee (fictional example)

## Model and research

- Model resume: two engineers at Jordan's level who get callbacks. Their pages run 7 entries and 13–14 bullets, nearly
  all into a second line, about three technical terms per bullet, ownership stated outright.
- Online research, Feb 2026: guidance read from three recruiter and hiring-manager sources. Two pages blocked (named here
  at the time).

| Date | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|

## Wording rejected (do not bring back)

- "Spearheaded the booking revamp"
- "Built the clinic scheduling platform"
- "Built the app patients use to book a doctor, so they get care faster" (clear, and tells an engineer nothing)
- "increasing developer velocity"
- "1 of 4 engineers" (team size says nothing about the work)

## Fixed facts (settled; do not re-ask)

- >80% is the post-visit survey on the new portal.
- ~2 minutes was measured by timing five changes; keep the "~".
- $1.2M is Finance's projection. Keep the word "projected".
- 94.2% is recall on Northgate's held-out test set.
